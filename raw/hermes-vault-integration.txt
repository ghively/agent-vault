    Integrated Hermes and its subagent swarm with Agent Vault as the
    centralized knowledge and credential source. Created skill
    agent-vault-integration with hermes_tools functions: vault_search,
    vault_lookup, vault_resolve_credential, vault_store, and
    vault_context_for_delegation. All credential resolution now goes
    through the vault (1P proxy) instead of direct op calls.
